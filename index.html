Eligibility & qualifications

Understand what your next designation requires.

Faculty eligibility depends on the applicable NMC regulations, recognized qualifications, teaching experience and other role-specific criteria. Use this overview as a starting point, then confirm your position against the latest official rules.

01

Recognized qualification

A relevant postgraduate medical qualification recognized for the specialty and proposed teaching role.

02

Teaching experience

Required experience at the appropriate designation and in a recognized medical institution.

03

Research contribution

Publications and academic work where required under the regulations applicable to the designation.

04

Current regulatory position

Eligibility reviewed against the latest Teacher Eligibility Qualifications and related official notifications.
